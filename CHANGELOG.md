# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## [Unreleased] - 2026-10-25

### Fixed
- Call hold now really holds the call. The holding phone sends silence, plays nothing and stops its video. The other phone recognises the hold offer (audio a=inactive/sendonly), answers a=inactive, does the same, and shows "<name> đang giữ máy". Resume brings the sound back and the picture with a fresh keyframe. If the other side rejects a hold or resume, the change is rolled back.
- SIP: a re-INVITE (hold, mute, opening an app) that arrives after the call has ended no longer rings as a new call. It is answered 481 Call/Transaction Does Not Exist, and the phone that sent it ends its side of the call on that 481.
- Call screen: Hold is greyed out while dialing or ringing and enabled once the call connects, as on a real phone. End, Mute and Speaker work throughout.
- Video sharing: vertical phone videos now show upright on the other phone. The sender passes the file's rotation tag in an RTP header extension, and the receiver's decoder applies it. Both phones also show the shared video at its real shape, centred, instead of stretched to full screen.
- Video sharing: smoother playback. The receiver now shows each frame at its own time, 200 ms behind the stream, with the sound delayed to match. The sender spreads each frame's packets out instead of bursting a whole keyframe, which used to be lost and freeze the picture.
- Call: the full-screen pill (green dot + timer) is bigger, darker and labelled "▲ Nút cuộc gọi", so it is clear that tapping it brings back the call buttons and the way out of the app.
- Dialer: the app opens on the phone tab with the keypad down and no leftover number; tapping a recent call or contact calls directly; after a call the screen returns to where it started (keypad cleared, recents, or contacts).
- **Apps would not open after ~2 h in a call (430):** the SIP registration (Expires 7200) was only renewed when the home screen resumed, so during a long call it expired and every Re-INVITE got 430 "Registration Lost". SipManager now renews it every hour on its own, re-registers at once on a 430, and opening an app retries the Re-INVITE once after that.
- **Call audio:** every call now starts with the mic unmuted (the mute button sets Android's global mic mute, which outlived the call, so a later call could send silence with the button showing off). Logs now show the mic and received sound level (`micPeak`, `rxPeak`) every 100 packets, plus the call volume at start.
- **No audio after the Busy Here change:** the 486 check also caught the call's own re-INVITEs (Call-ID not the one stored), so the setup re-INVITEs were refused and both sides were silent. Only an INVITE without a To-tag (a new call) can now get 486.
- **Chia sẻ vị trí:** with the phone's Location switch off, live sharing said "Đang chờ GPS" forever; it now says "Vị trí đang tắt · Bật Vị trí trong cài đặt nhanh" (and recovers once Location is turned on).
- Calls left hanging on the other phone: (1) when the app crashes during a call it now sends BYE (CANCEL while still ringing out) before it dies; (2) the call screen was never meant to be rebuilt, but Android rebuilt it after a configuration change (e.g. coming back from a landscape app during a screen share), and that path skipped the BYE through a bug (`onDestroy` set `isEndingCall` before checking it). The call screen now handles configuration changes itself and the BYE is sent; (3) as a last resort a phone ends the call after 20 s without any call audio from the other phone (not for hotlines), with a "Mất kết nối" toast.
- Content Sharing video, the receiver still froze for seconds: video packets arrive slightly out of order through the MF (log: `Lost 4 … before seq 36835` then `Lost 65526 … before seq 36831`), and every reordering counted as a loss that dropped all pictures until the next keyframe. The receiver now puts packets back in order (64-packet window) before rebuilding pictures, and a real loss drops only the damaged picture (it waits for a keyframe only when the damaged picture was one).
- Content Sharing video, the receiver lagged: one thread both read the RTP socket and fed the decoder, so packets overflowed the small default socket buffer while the decoder was busy, and every lost packet froze the picture until the next keyframe. Receiving and decoding now run on separate threads, the content sockets use 4 MB receive / 1 MB send buffers, decoded frames are shown as soon as they are ready, and a decoder that falls a second behind skips to the next keyframe. Lost packets are logged (`ContentReceiver: Lost N video packet(s)`).
- Content Sharing video: the call kept its camera/microphone RTP on the first audio/video pair instead of switching to the shared-content pair a Re-INVITE adds (the receiver flickered between the shared video and the camera, and the sender's call video froze once the share closed and that pair went to port 0). The shared video now draws on a plain surface layer under the self-camera tile, with the call video hidden behind it, so the camera no longer mixes into it.
- **Content Sharing, reopening it showed "… muốn chia sẻ ảnh" on the other phone and "Không mở được ứng dụng" here:** leaving the Content Sharing page with Back or the menu (not Thoát) kept the share open locally (`shareStream = true`), so the next App Re-INVITE (opening Content Sharing again) still carried `a=dcmap:1002`. The other phone took it for a share request, answered 180 and waited for its user, this phone got no 200 OK within 5 s and rolled the App line back. Switching away from the page now drops the local share (or cancels a pending request), and an incoming offer that opens or swaps the App line is never treated as a share request.
- **Content Sharing, photo bytes never reached the other phone (MF):** the App-slot data connection (`-app` sessions, built by `initSessionAdcPC`) opened only streams 1000 and 1001, so the photo pieces the phone sent on stream 1002 had no channel on the MF and were dropped without a log line. It now opens 1000/1001/1002 from `adcStreamIDs` like the other ADC path, and accepts an announced 1002 too.
- **Content Sharing, photos stay blurred with the progress line running:** ending a share closed the content-share data channel (stream 1002). That reset the SCTP stream, the MF closed its own 1002 for the phone (`ADC3 = nil`) and never reopened it, so on every later share in the same call the offer, thumbnails and page turns (stream 1000) still arrived but the full-size photo bytes were dropped. The channel now stays open for the life of the App line and is reused by each share; it closes only with the App PeerConnection.
- **Content Sharing, stuck waiting and "Không thể chia sẻ lúc này":** a photo share is now accepted only when the 200 OK carries `a=dcmap:1002`; an answer without it (a phone on an old build) closes the share and shows "Máy kia chưa hỗ trợ chia sẻ nội dung" (code 505) instead of opening the picker. Hủy after the other phone accepted, or closing the picker without choosing, now leaves the session (`stopShare`) so the next Ảnh works. The wait screen reads "Đang mở thư viện…" once accepted, and a phone whose app has no `requestShare` shows "Cần cập nhật ứng dụng trên máy này" instead of waiting for ever. Added an empty `_handleMessageFromNative` hook and silenced the `setCameraTile` warning.
- Added log lines on the photo path (share channel attach/receive, `streamFile start`, `photo assembled`, share events sent to the page) to locate where a transfer stops.

### Added & Enhanced
- Standby mode: the app stays reachable for calls with the screen off or the app closed. A foreground service with a "Sẵn sàng nhận cuộc gọi" notification owns the incoming-call handler, re-registers every ~4 min from an allow-while-idle alarm, restarts SIP after a process restart or reboot, and asks once for the battery-optimisation exemption and full-screen-call permission. A REGISTER that gets no reply in 4 s now reopens the SIP connection, which fixes "the app is open but nobody can call" after a long sleep.
- Video sharing: H.265 (HEVC) videos can now be shared as they are, without conversion (RTP payload type 98, RFC 7798). The MF relay needs no change; both phones decode H.265 in hardware.
- Home screen restyled like the One UI Phone app: floating pill tabs Bàn phím · Gần đây · Danh bạ, a full-screen keypad, a large "5G New Calling" title, and rounded card lists grouped by day (recents, with "(n)" for repeated calls) or by letter (contacts).
- **Camera drag only on the call screen:** my camera tile can be dragged only on the call screen itself (emote home). Inside any app (Chia sẻ and everything in it, Vẽ, Caro, …) it stays where the app puts it; tapping still folds it where folding is allowed.
- **Chia sẻ vị trí back:** pressing back on the map now closes it on the other phone too (`map.close` over the ADC, toast "… đã đóng chia sẻ vị trí"), like stopping a photo/video share.
- **Chia sẻ vị trí invite:** choosing "Chia sẻ vị trí" now shows the other phone the same accept/decline popup as photo/video/screen (it used to open the map there straight away); the sender waits, can cancel, and both maps open on accept. Page-only, over the existing ADC.
- **Busy Here:** a call that arrives while the phone is already in a call or ringing, or a call to your own number, gets 486 Busy Here; the current call is untouched (a second INVITE used to take over its state). The caller sees and hears "Người dùng đang bận" (Vietnamese TTS, busy tone if no voice).
- Chia sẻ video: the video's sound plays at 30% on both phones (sender's preview and receiver), so it no longer drowns out the other person's voice. Like the voice, it follows the call's earpiece/speaker choice.
- Chia sẻ màn hình: the viewer no longer gets a speaker button (a screen share has no sound, so it did nothing).
- Chia sẻ menu (and its waiting screens): my camera and the other person sit side by side in a row at the top, like Caro (34% of the width each, 3:4, no folding or dragging), with the menu under it. The photo, video and screen-share views keep the small corner tiles (my camera at the top left, the other person under it), which fold on tap. The page tells native which layout it wants (`AndroidBridge.setCameraRow`).
- Chia sẻ: the menu (and the waiting screens) is full screen too, like the photo and video views: the title sits beside my camera, the choices low on the screen above the call pill. Tap the pill for the call buttons.
- Chia sẻ, while a video or screen is shared: the other person's camera tile stays on (under my camera) instead of hiding, on both phones. Tap it to fold it as usual.
- Chia sẻ video: the sender now hears the video too (before, their copy played silent). The page's mute button shows for the sender as well and silences only their phone; on speakerphone the other person may hear an echo through the call, so mute it or use earphones. The mute setting also carries over to the next shared video on the receiver.
- Caro fills tall screens: in full screen the two video tiles grow into the height that was left empty under the board (up to the pair spanning the width, 12dp side margins), and the page centres the board in its panel instead of pinning it to the top.
- App-open request: the "<name> muốn mở <app>" prompt is now a centred card (rounded on all sides, 24dp from the screen edges, at most 400dp wide, dialog pop-in animation) instead of a bottom sheet.
- Fix: the other person's camera tile in Vẽ & Chia sẻ was never visible — it reused the background call video, which lies under the page, so the white page painted over it. The tile is now its own SurfaceView in the call UI layer (media overlay, brought to front like my camera) and the decoder switches between it and the background; the hide-and-recreate z-order workaround is gone.
- Long-call video/audio stability: decoded frames render immediately (they were scheduled at the far phone's camera clock, which could hold every frame up to 1 s and stall the decoder); a decoder that errors is recreated on the same surface instead of staying black, and a release racing the receiver thread no longer leaks or kills the new decoder; returning to the call screen keeps a working decoder instead of rebuilding it (a freeze until the next keyframe); a new decoder asks for a keyframe; idle decoded frames are pulled out every 20 ms; video RTP payloads cap at 1200 bytes (no IP fragmentation over VPN/mobile MTUs); audio is written without blocking so clock drift between phones can no longer build up a growing voice delay (a first version capped the queue below the track's buffer size, so playback never started: no audio); encoder input slots are always returned; a disposed camera track no longer aborts stop() before the sockets close.
- Calls in the background: the incoming-call heads-up now pops up only when the app is not on screen (posted when you leave the ringing screen, removed when you come back; the ringtone is unchanged). Requests from the other phone ("muốn mở 🎨 Vẽ", "muốn chia sẻ ảnh/video") get a heads-up when the call screen is hidden, with Đồng ý (opens the call and accepts) and Từ chối. The camera keeps sending while the app is in the background (call service now also has the camera type, started when the call is placed or answered, so the mic also survives a call that connects after pressing Home); without that type it pauses as before.
- Vẽ & Chia sẻ show the other person's camera: a 90×125 tile under my camera (left column; the pages keep the top-right for their buttons). A tap folds it into a round button with their initial, a tap on the button opens it again, like my camera tile. ### Fixed
- Switching straight from one data-channel app to another (e.g. emote home → Caro, or back) left the new app on "Đang chờ kết nối" and broke the old one: the IMS-AS patched the open MF ADC context in place, and the MF ignores a new ADC offer once its connection is established. The phone now releases the current app's ADC first (Re-INVITE for operator-menu, waits up to 5 s for its 200 OK), then opens the new app with a fresh ADC.
- Photo-share showed the shared photo zoomed in on the sender and small on the receiver: the page never fitted the image to the viewer, so the sender's 1920 px photo overflowed the clipped viewer and the receiver's 256 px thumbnail stayed at its natural size. The page now computes a contain-fit size for every photo (thumb or full) when it loads.
- Photo-share "Chọn ảnh" froze the app: the WebView had no file chooser (`onShowFileChooser`), so the button did nothing, and a photo that did get through was decoded at full size (a 50 MP ARGB bitmap is ~200 MB) after being sent through the JS bridge as tens of MB of base64. The call screen now opens the system image picker, the page downsizes photos to 1920 px JPEG before sending, and native decoding uses `inSampleSize`.
- Photo-share showed `ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED` when opened from the operator-menu: it was the only mini-app not bundled in the APK, and its package download over the BDC was cut off by the app's own Re-INVITE (which closes the BDC) before the zip arrived. A bundled copy now exists at `assets/miniapps/photo-share/` (identical to `go-mf/static/apps/photo-share/`) and is used until a downloaded copy is cached.
- Hotline app re-INVITE (e.g. 1900 → flight-booking): IMS-AS answered the phone with the callee-leg ADC credentials, so the ADC stayed CONNECTING and DCAS never got search/select/confirm. The answer now carries MF's caller-side ADC transport.
- Declining an incoming call from the notification left MT on the ringing screen while MO had already hung up. Declining now ends the call locally on MT too.
- Video sent from some phones (e.g. OPPO / MediaTek) showed black-and-white on the far end: the encoder is now fed through `getInputImage()` so each chip's colour layout is honoured.
- Choppy received video: incomplete FU-A NALUs are dropped instead of decoded, the decoder gets more time for input, the video socket buffer is larger, and outgoing RTP now has correct sequence numbers and marker bits.
- Smeared/blocky video after packet loss: the receiver now counts every RTP gap and freezes on the last good frame until the next keyframe instead of decoding broken frames; failed sends are logged and the video socket send buffer is larger.
- Blocky video on movement: the network delivers video RTP out of order (e.g. 1504, 1506, 1507, 1505), and fragments were joined in arrival order. The receiver now holds early packets for up to 60 ms and puts them back in sequence order. Only a gap that never fills counts as loss and triggers the keyframe freeze.
- Long freezes after real packet loss: the receiver now sends an RTCP PLI keyframe request (repeated every 300 ms while frozen) and the sender forces a keyframe, so video recovers in about 0.1 s instead of up to 1 s. Large frames are also paced (1 ms pause every 4 packets) so keyframe bursts are not dropped by Wi-Fi/VPN queues.
- Video sent from OPPO / MediaTek phones (Reno13) was grey with a green band on the right edge on the far end, even though the encoder input was correct: the MediaTek hardware H.264 encoder is now replaced by the software encoder on those phones, and SPS/PPS are repeated before every keyframe because the software encoder only emits them once.
- No audio or video on either phone when an OPPO was the callee: the phone's own IMS stack held UDP 40008, the fixed callee video port, so binding failed and the app ran the call with no RTP sockets. The app now uses OS-assigned free RTP ports per call (announced in the SDP), and a failed bind to the announced IP now really falls back to the wildcard address (the retry reused the socket the failed bind had closed).
- Calls froze when the phone's IP changed (Wi-Fi <-> mobile data, new carrier IP, VPN reconnect): SIP and RTP stayed on the old IP. The app now watches the default network; 1.5 s after a change it compares the IP used to reach the SIP server. If it changed, SIP reconnects and re-REGISTERs from the new IP, and a call in progress is ended cleanly (BYE over the new connection, so the other phone hangs up too, plus a "Mạng thay đổi" toast).
- Garbled (mojibake) Vietnamese error messages sent by DCAS for invalid Caro moves.
